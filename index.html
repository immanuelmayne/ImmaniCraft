How To Start

Click inside the game, then use `Arrow Left`, `Arrow Right`, and `Arrow Up`.

You can play offline with no Wi-Fi. Solo play and local split-screen work right from this page.

Left click mines a block. Right click places the selected block or opens a door.

Click the page once to start the built-in ambient game music and sound effects.

Water lets you swim. Lava hurts you in Survival, so watch your hearts.

Stay underwater too long in Survival and you will drown, so come up for air.

Beds are 2 blocks tall. Right click a bed at night or during a thunder storm to sleep until morning or until the storm is over.

In Creative, tap `Space` two quick times to fly, then use `Space` up and `Shift` down.

Monsters take 10 hits to defeat. Villagers wander around villages during the day.

Thunder storms now last longer, flash across the sky, and do not hurt you.

Animals and sea animals now spawn in the world. Pick the `Lead` item and right click them to leash them.

Player 2 uses `A`, `D`, and `W` to move, `F` to mine in front, and `G` to place in front.

Player 3 uses `J`, `L`, and `I` to move, `U` to mine, and `O` to place. Player 4 uses numpad `4`, `6`, `8`, `7`, and `9`.

Nether portals are 2 blocks tall now. Step into one to switch between the Overworld and the Nether.

Snow, ice, buckets, and water buckets are in the world now too.

Use the `Full Screen` button for a bigger split-screen view.

Game Info
  • Natural wood-and-leaf trees that spawn on their own
  • Water, lava, snow, ice, volcanoes, nether portals, rain, longer thunder storms, and a day-to-night world cycle
  • Doors and nether portals use 2-block-tall structures, with portal travel into the Nether
  • A much bigger looping world with larger villages, brown-robed long-nosed villagers, stronger faux-3D depth, animals, sea animals, leads, buckets, food and water stations, and local 1-4 player split-screen

Online Multiplayer

Offline play works right away with no Wi-Fi. This section is only for optional shared-room play.

Host a room or join a shared world. Everyone in a room sees the same map and block changes.

Offline mode works with no Wi-Fi. If you want shared rooms later, run `npm start` and use `http://localhost:3000`.
